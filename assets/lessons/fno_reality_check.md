# What the data says about F&O traders

In September 2024 SEBI published a study of individual traders in equity F&O between FY22 and FY24. Its findings:

- About **93% of individual F&O traders lost money** over the three years.
- Their **average loss was around ₹2 lakh** per trader, including transaction costs.
- Only about **1% earned more than ₹1 lakh** over the period, after costs.
- Combined losses of individual traders were well over **₹1.8 lakh crore** in three years.

Follow-up data has shown the same pattern. Read the SEBI study yourself on sebi.gov.in.

## Why the odds are so bad

1. **Costs**: every trade pays fees and the spread. Small edges disappear.
2. **Theta**: option buyers fight time decay every single day.
3. **Leverage**: normal volatility becomes account-ending.
4. **Who's on the other side**: proprietary trading firms and algorithms with better data, speed and risk control.
5. **Behaviour**: overtrading, revenge trades after a loss, and holding losers hoping they'll recover.

## SEBI's response

Since late 2024 SEBI has raised contract sizes, cut the number of weekly expiries, and required upfront premium collection, all to curb retail speculation.

## A healthy rule of thumb

- Build your long-term plan first: emergency fund, insurance and SIPs.
- If you want to learn F&O, use **paper trading**, or a small amount you are fully prepared to lose (say 1–2% of your portfolio), and only with **defined-risk** strategies.
- Use options mainly to **protect** what you own, not to generate income.

Knowing how F&O works makes you a better investor, even if you never trade it.
